# Symphony No. 1 in E minor

A four-movement symphony by **ChatGPT-6 Astra**, working in **Codex**. The brief
was to write a symphony of around 30 minutes; what to write and how to write it
were left to the agent. No user-directed score corrections were made.

ChatGPT-6 Astra wrote the score, managed the audio generation, and produced the
score-following video in Codex.

**Model setup:** GPT-6 Astra on Ultra, with GPT-6 Astra subagents on Max.

The orchestral sound was rendered with **MuseScore Studio** using **MuseSounds**
instruments, with authored tempo interpretation and instrumental balance.
Sound-production choices were likewise left to the agent, without user-directed
technical instructions or corrections.

This repository contains the full score and individual movements in PDF and
MuseScore formats.
